40k Caddie privacy

40k Caddie is a free, personal Warhammer 40,000 game companion made by a hobbyist for himself and his friends. It is not a Games Workshop product.

What it keeps

Everything you enter (armies, opponents, battle history, images you add) is saved on your device. If you sign in, it is also saved to your own account so it follows you to other devices. Signing in with Google shares your name and email address with the app; nothing else from your Google account is read.

Where it is kept

Account data is stored with Supabase, the database service the app uses. Only you can read or change your data. Images you add to your account are stored as files that anyone with their exact web address could open.

What it does not do

No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of your data.

Removing your data

Ask the app's owner (the contact email on the Google sign-in screen) and your account and data will be deleted.
