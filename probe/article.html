Thoughts

Two unique builds face off in the final, which is just what we want to see! In the black and red corner, we've got an extremely toolboxy Martyrs Sisters build, which seems practically designed to delight me with how many options it has. In the green corner, Orks, sporting Zzap Gunwagons in place of some of the standard shooting pieces, creating a very crunchy target profile for foes to figure out, and also plenty of reach. The matchup is Priority vs. Purge, so the Sisters are trying to force through a count to 45 by Actioning centrally, while Purge needs to try and (deff)roll them off the table. Who wins?

In the Sisters favour, they're Martyrs so they can revive Characters, which gives them plenty of depth, and are set up to massively punish the Orks if they overextend (as the Triumph and Vahl between them provide incredible counterattack potential), so stops them from being aggressive they might like. They also have quite dense units, so picking stuff off for Kill More is non-trivial for the Orks. That cuts both ways though - it also means the Orks don't have to kill that many units before it starts really hurting for the Sisters in terms of keeping Actions or board presence going. Who comes off better?

Realistically, both these lists can win the game. I love Zzap guns in general, but they and Tankbustas don't have a massive number of ideal targets here, so the Sisters can prioritise other targets. On the other hand, the Orks are definitely going to kill some stuff, and every unit the Sisters lose sets them back.

As always when such things are streamed, I had a quick skim through the video, and the game does indeed look to have been very close. The game was on the Dawn of War layout for this mission, and Jean-Loup made the smart decision to deploy very turtled up in one corner. This forced the Orks to come and get him, and staggered when they arrived, and while they did so with gusto, they couldn't fully sweep the Sisters at speed. From there, it looks like Vahl refused to die on a really pivotal turn, giving the Sisters the momentum to take the game from that point. Well done to both players for making it into the finals in such a spectacular event, and congratulations to Jean-Loup.
